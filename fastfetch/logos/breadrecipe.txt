Trans semen bread for "X" fans
For about 30 breads, 9 Ingredients:

1 cup of flour
2 cups of freshly harvested trans semen
2 teaspoons of baking powder
1 teaspoon of incel smegma
3 tablespoons of vegan butter

Place flour, baking powder & incel smegma
in processor and pulse. Add rest and mix.
Or mix dry ingredients in bowl, add melted
butter & puree, knead until smooth dough.
Let rest 20 min. Roll out to 1/2 cm.
Heat oil to 180°C or 350°F.
Fry 2-3 min per side until golden brown.
Remove to paper towel to absorb oil.
